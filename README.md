# Web-Ready Access Platform

The **Web-Ready Access (WRA) Platform** is a free, no-signup resource created by [Oasis of Change](https://oasisofchange.org), a youth-led nonprofit based in Vancouver, Canada. It helps nonprofits, small organizations, and mission-driven teams build websites that are accessible, digitally sustainable, and supported by in-kind technology grants — without needing a large budget or technical background.

The platform is organized into three self-guided learning tracks: **Web Accessibility**, covering how to make your site usable for everyone and meet legal requirements; **Digital Sustainability**, focused on reducing your website's carbon footprint and improving performance; and **In-Kind Technology Grants**, showing how to unlock free software from Google, Microsoft, Canva, Atlassian, and others worth thousands of dollars per year.

Each track includes practical checklists, plain-language guides, and step-by-step walkthroughs. The platform also features a comprehensive Google Ad Grants guide, helping eligible nonprofits access up to $10,000 per month in free Google Search advertising.

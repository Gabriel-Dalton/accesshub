About the WRA Hub

The Web-Ready Access (WRA) Hub is a free resource created by Oasis of Change, a youth-led nonprofit focused on digital sustainability. The Hub helps nonprofits, small organizations, and mission-driven teams build websites that are sustainable, accessible, and supported by in-kind technology grants — all without needing a big budget or a technical background.

Whether you are a community organization just getting started online or an established nonprofit looking to improve your digital presence, this playbook is for you. You will find clear, jargon-free guides on three pillars:

  1. Digital Sustainability — How to reduce your website's carbon footprint and build an energy-efficient online presence
  2. Web Accessibility — How to make your website usable for everyone, including people with disabilities, and understand the legal requirements
  3. In-Kind Technology Grants — How to unlock free software and tools from companies like Google, Microsoft, Canva, and Atlassian

At Oasis of Change, we have helped nonprofits secure over $865,000 in in-kind technology grants based on the maximum eligible value of the programs listed. We have also achieved up to 98% reductions in website carbon emissions for our clients. If a youth-led organization can do it, your team can do it too.

Disclaimer: All dollar figures related to in-kind technology grants in this guide represent the maximum eligible value of the programs listed, based on commercial pricing equivalents at the time of writing (February 2026). Actual value depends on your organization's size, usage, and which products you activate. Program terms, pricing, and availability may change at the discretion of the providing companies. Always check the official program pages for the most current information.

Recommended order

Start with accessibility foundations, optimize sustainability next, then package outcomes into stronger tech-grant applications.

1. Accessibility first

Fix contrast, semantics, focus order, and form labeling. Improves outcomes immediately and is usually fast to deliver.

2. Sustainability next

About this resource

The Web-Ready Access Hub was created by Oasis of Change Inc., a federal not-for-profit corporation based in Vancouver, Canada. Oasis of Change is a youth-led organization focused on digital sustainability and environmental impact reduction, founded by Gabriel Dalton.

This resource was developed with support from a Government of Canada grant through Service Canada, as part of Oasis of Change's mission to equip nonprofits with the practical tools they need to thrive in the digital world.

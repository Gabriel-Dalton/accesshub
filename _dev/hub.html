Overview

Sustainable, Accessible, and Grant-Supported Solutions for Nonprofits

The Web-Ready Access Platform is a free resource created by Oasis of Change, a youth-led nonprofit focused on digital sustainability. It helps nonprofits, small organizations, and mission-driven teams build websites that are sustainable, accessible, and supported by in-kind technology grants: all without needing a big budget or a technical background.

Digital Sustainability

Reduce your website's carbon footprint, lower hosting costs, and build a faster, energy-efficient online presence.

Web Accessibility

Make your website usable for everyone, including people with disabilities, and understand the legal requirements.

Tech Grants

Unlock free software and tools from Google, Microsoft, Canva, and more. Learn how to apply for nonprofit technology grants.

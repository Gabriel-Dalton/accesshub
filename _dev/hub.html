Overview

Sustainable, Accessible, and Grant-Supported Solutions for Nonprofits

The Web-Ready Access Hub is a free resource created by Oasis of Change, a youth-led nonprofit focused on digital sustainability. It helps nonprofits, small organizations, and mission-driven teams build websites that are sustainable, accessible, and supported by in-kind technology grants — all without needing a big budget or a technical background.

Digital Sustainability

How to reduce your website's carbon footprint and build an energy-efficient online presence.

Web Accessibility

How to make your website usable for everyone, including people with disabilities, and understand the legal requirements.

In-Kind Technology Grants

How to unlock free software and tools from companies like Google, Microsoft, Canva, and Atlassian.

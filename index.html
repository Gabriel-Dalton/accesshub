The cost of waiting

Every month without an accessible, well-performing web presence costs real things — grant eligibility, volunteer hours spent on broken forms, and supporters who leave because they can’t use your site. These guides are free. The cost of waiting is not.

What’s inside

Web accessibility

Inaccessible sites turn away donors, volunteers, and people who need your services — and they carry real legal risk. Get clear, jargon-free guidance on making your site usable for everyone.

Digital sustainability

Heavy, slow sites waste server energy and lose people before the page loads. You pay more in hosting and get less in return. Learn practical ways to reduce your site's footprint and improve load times.

Tech grants

Google, Microsoft, Canva, and others give nonprofits free software worth hundreds of thousands per year. If your organization isn’t applying, you’re either paying for tools you could have for free — or going without them entirely.

About WRA Hub

The Web-Ready Access Hub is a youth-led program hosted by Oasis of Change. It combines three pillars—accessibility, sustainability, and funding support—to help nonprofits expand their reach, operate efficiently, and serve their communities more inclusively.

Our mission:

Equip Canadian nonprofits with the knowledge, tools, and connections needed to build accessible, sustainable web presences and unlock in-kind technology grants.

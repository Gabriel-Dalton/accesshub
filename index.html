Web-Ready Access Hub

Technical justification for WRA Hub equipment procurement.

This report supports the acquisition of core technology to deliver a free, centralized digital resource for Canadian nonprofits. The WRA Hub is a youth-led initiative hosted by Oasis of Change, funded by Canada Service Corps at Employment and Social Development Canada and administered by the GLOCAL Foundation of Canada through the CANConnect Micro-grant Diversity program.

Why procurement matters

Equipment enables a resilient hub that can prototype, audit, and publish accessible sites at speed while training youth leaders and nonprofit partners to sustain their digital presence.

Free

Centralized access to tools, templates, and guidance.

Accessible

Inclusive design and compliance-first build standards.

Sustainable

Long-term capacity building for nonprofit teams.

Project objectives

Centralize support

Deliver a one-stop hub for Canadian nonprofits to access digital strategy, accessible templates, and in-kind tech grant guidance.

Build skills

Train youth leaders and nonprofit staff to maintain accessible, secure, and sustainable web platforms.

Expand reach

Strengthen digital inclusion by helping organizations serve their communities with modern, multilingual websites.

Technical justification highlights

Research & audits

Hardware supports accessibility testing, performance analysis, and inclusive user research sessions led by youth teams.

Production workflows

Reliable equipment streamlines design, content, and deployment for multiple nonprofit partners in parallel.

Community delivery

A shared tech stack ensures the hub remains available, secure, and scalable for future cohorts and partner organizations.

Implementation timeline

Phase 01: Procurement & setup

Acquire equipment, configure secure environments, and validate accessibility testing tools.

Phase 02: Hub build & training

Launch core hub resources, onboard nonprofit partners, and deliver youth-led skill sessions.

Phase 03: Scale & sustain

Expand partner network, refine resources, and secure long-term in-kind technology grant pathways.

About WRA Hub

The Web-Ready Access Hub is hosted by Oasis of Change and powered by Canada Service Corps and Employment and Social Development Canada, with program administration by the GLOCAL Foundation of Canada.

Project focus:

A free, centralized digital resource that enables accessible, sustainable websites and unlocks essential in-kind technology grants for Canadian nonprofits.

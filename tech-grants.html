Track 03

Nonprofit tech grants documentation

Build a repeatable grant process, not one-off applications. This page structures how teams identify opportunities, prepare materials, and improve quality each cycle.

Opportunity mapping

Create and maintain a grant pipeline:

  • List grants by deadline, funding type, and effort required
  • Tag opportunities by mission fit and expected impact
  • Use quarterly reviews to retire low-fit programs

Readiness library

Keep key documents ready before application season:

  • Mission summary, impact metrics, and financial snapshots
  • Governance, safeguarding, and privacy policies
  • One-page implementation plans for requested support

Application quality

Improve consistency with internal review:

  • Score drafts for clarity, evidence, and feasibility
  • Collect reusable language from successful submissions
  • Track outcomes and rejection reasons to refine strategy
